Photo Review

A local review HUD for Google Photos.

Review where your photos already are

Accept, reject and rate photos directly in Google Photos. Your decisions and photo links stay in this browser.

  1. Name your local review library. Use a different name for each Google account.
  2. Open a photo and use Accept, Reject or a 1–5 star rating.
  3. Return to the grid, choose a review filter and select loaded matches.

The HUD only selects photos it can identify and confirm. Unloaded or unsupported items are skipped. No photos are uploaded, moved, edited or deleted.

After an update, reload your Google Photos tab once. Older Lightroom review data is preserved separately; this workflow does not use Lightroom.
